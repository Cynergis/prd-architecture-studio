# The studio: the arc, end to end

Load **atlas** and run its scenes in order, stopping at every gate for the person: scene 1
(start: product type, what they have, the project, the packs), scene 2 (specify, through
**capture**), scene 3 (derive the domain: OTO's **start** on the specification, then
**ontology-interview**), scene 4 (design, through **capture**), scene 6 (publish). Scenes 5 and 7
are what happens after: a feature through **feature-flow**, a reader with a pack.

Nothing in this skill does work of its own; Atlas does, with the skills it coordinates.
